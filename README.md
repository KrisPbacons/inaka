# Inaka Town

**Inaka Town** is a cozy pixel-art town management and life simulation game built with the Godot Engine. 
Players take on the role of the mayor of a small rural Japanese town and guide it from a quiet village into a thriving community while preserving its traditions and natural beauty.

Inspired by games such as **Stardew Valley**, **Animal Crossing**, and **The Sims**, Inaka Town offers a relaxing experience where players can build, explore and shape the future of their town at their own pace.

## Features

- 🌸 Relaxing pixel-art gameplay inspired by rural Japan
- 🏘️ Build and upgrade homes, businesses, and public facilities
- 🌾 Farming, fishing, gathering, and local production
- 😊 Dynamic NPCs with dialogue, requests, and relationships
- 🎎 Seasonal festivals and changing weather
- 🏯 Traditional Japanese-inspired architecture and environments
- 🌳 Explore forests, rivers, caves, and hidden locations
- 🐻 Wildlife encounters and rare mythical creatures
- 💰 Grow the local economy through tourism, trade, and town development
- 🚉 Balance modernization with preserving the town's cultural heritage

## Built With

- **Engine:** Godot 4
- **Language:** GDScript
- **Art Style:** Handcrafted 32-bit pixel art
- **Platform:** Windows (planned cross-platform support)
